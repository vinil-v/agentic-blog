## Feature Story: The Rise of AI-Enhanced HPC

HPC and AI workloads are rapidly converging. Scientific teams increasingly use machine learning to guide simulations, identify promising experiments, and analyze massive datasets. At the same time, synthetic data generated by HPC applications is helping researchers train specialized AI models.

This convergence is changing infrastructure requirements. Organizations are investing in unified platforms that support large-scale simulation, distributed model training, high-throughput inference, and fast data movement. Open software ecosystems and portable programming models remain essential as hardware becomes more diverse.

## Practical Tip: Improve Accelerator Utilization

Before purchasing additional hardware, review how existing resources are used:

1. Measure queue times, idle periods, and memory utilization.
2. Profile data-loading and preprocessing bottlenecks.
3. Consolidate small inference jobs where appropriate.
4. Use mixed precision after validating numerical accuracy.
5. Track energy consumption alongside performance metrics.
